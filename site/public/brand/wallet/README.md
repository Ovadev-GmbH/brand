# Add to Wallet buttons

The badges that save a ticket to a phone's wallet, in German and English.

| File | Button |
|---|---|
| `apple-wallet-de.svg`, `apple-wallet-en.svg` | Hinzufügen zu Apple Wallet / Add to Apple Wallet |
| `google-wallet-de.svg`, `google-wallet-en.svg` | Hinzufügen zu Google Wallet / Add to Google Wallet |

Use the badge in the page's language, unaltered: no recolouring, no cropping, no text of our own on it. Keep it at least 30 px high with clear space of half its height around it. Show both badges side by side, Apple first, or only the one that matches the visitor's phone.

The badges are Apple's and Google's artwork and trademarks, used under the [Apple Wallet identity guidelines](https://developer.apple.com/wallet/add-to-apple-wallet-guidelines/) and the [Google Wallet brand guidelines](https://developers.google.com/wallet/generic/resources/brand-guidelines). What the passes themselves look like is in [`/ticketova/tickets/`](../../ticketova/tickets/README.md).
